PROJECT Z / APP SETUP

Put Z on your Home Screen.

Follow these instructions to add Z's app icon to your phone. It's a web app, so you don't need an App Store download.

iPhone · Safari

Add Z on iPhone

1
Open Z in Safari.

If you're viewing it inside another app, use its menu to open the link in Safari first.

2
Tap the Share icon.

It's the square with an upward-pointing arrow in Safari's toolbar.

3
Choose Add to Home Screen.

You may need to scroll down the Share menu to find this option.

4
Tap Add.

Z's icon will appear on your Home Screen, ready to open.

Android · Chrome

Add Z on Android

1
Open Z in Chrome.
2
Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).

It's near the address bar.

3
Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Confirm the installation if Chrome asks you.

If Z is already installed and you're reading this inside the Z app, you're all set. To add it again on another phone, open Z's website in that phone's browser.
