    Match the actual sound.

    Sound DNA measures rhythm, energy, bass, brightness, texture and movement from audio files you choose. Then it finds tracks that sound closest within your analyzed library — never by streams or popularity.

    Choose up to 5 files at once (maximum 16 MB each) that you own or have permission to analyze. Name files “Artist - Song.mp3” for clearer results. Analysis happens on your device, and only the sound measurements are saved here. Nothing is uploaded.

    Add two or more tracks to discover actual sonic matches.

    Optional: add your own words, separated by commas, such as hypnotic, nostalgic, majestic, peaceful, or bittersweet. Z treats these as your feelings, not universal facts.

    Audio analysis is approximate and cannot determine every emotion, lyric meaning, melody or chord from a file. Labels are listening hypotheses, not facts about how you feel. Apple and Spotify audio are never downloaded for analysis; catalog suggestions use artist and genre clues only unless you provide analyzable audio.

    Catalog discovery uses artist and genre clues, not popularity scores; it does not analyze Apple, Spotify, or Audius audio automatically. Sound DNA only compares audio you explicitly choose on your device. Clips promote songs in the store; music-related feelings vary by listener.
