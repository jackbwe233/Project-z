Z

Z on your Lock Screen.

Give Z a single-tap entry point on your iPhone using Apple's built-in Shortcuts app.

1 · Create your Z shortcut

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap + to create a shortcut, and name it Z.
  3. Add the action Open URLs (or a URL action followed by Open URLs).
  4. Paste the Z voice-launch URL below as the URL.
  5. Choose a Z icon or a simple letter-style icon, then run it once to test.
https://jackbwe233.github.io/Project-z/quick.html

Try it now →

2 · Put it on your Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold your iPhone Lock Screen and tap Customize.
  2. Pick the Lock Screen and choose an available Shortcuts control or widget.
  3. Select the Z shortcut you just made.
  4. Tap Done. Tapping it opens Z's voice page.

The exact control names depend on your iOS version and Lock Screen layout. A website can't install a custom native Lock Screen widget itself.

3 · Add the Z app to your Home Screen

  1. Open Z Chat in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Enable Open as Web App if offered, then tap Add.

Z already has an app manifest and icon. It should appear as a standalone web app on compatible devices.

How voice works now

Press the microphone and speak to save a note or add a reminder. Automatic always-listening voice chat, lock-screen microphone listening, and native Lock Screen widgets require different platform capabilities and aren't implemented here.
