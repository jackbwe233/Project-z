Connect Google Calendar

Read your next events in Z with Google's official authorization window. You choose whether to grant access.

Google Calendar

Not connected

This connection is read-only. Z can't create, change, or delete your Google events. No Google password is entered into Z.

To connect, configure a Google OAuth client below and click Connect Google.

Z saves only your public OAuth client ID to this browser. Access tokens are kept in memory, are not written to localStorage, and disappear when you close this page. If you reopen it, connect again.

Upcoming events

Connect to see your Google Calendar events.

Copying events is optional. It saves a one-time snapshot on this device, not a live sync. Z won't update or remove those copies when your Google Calendar changes.

One-time Google setup

Google requires the website owner to configure an OAuth client before sign-in will work. You'll need access to a Google Cloud project:

  1. Open Google Cloud credentials and select or create a project.
  2. Enable the Google Calendar API for that project.
  3. Configure the Google Auth Platform / OAuth consent screen. If your app is in testing, add your own Google account as an authorized test user.
  4. Create an OAuth client with application type Web application.
  5. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, enter https://jackbwe233.github.io (no /Project-z path).
  6. Copy the Client ID (not a client secret), paste it above, and click Connect Google.

Your Google Workspace school administrator can block third-party connections. Public distribution or sensitive scopes may require Google's verification. Don't paste a client secret or an API key here.

Current scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly.
