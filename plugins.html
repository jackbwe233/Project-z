Quick actions

📅 Calendar reminders

Download your Z tasks as a calendar file with alerts: 15 minutes before timed tasks, or at 9 AM for all-day tasks. Import it into Apple Calendar and allow Calendar notifications to receive alerts when Z is closed. Importing a second time may duplicate events in some apps.

📤 Share with Messages or apps

Open your iPhone share sheet to send Z's link to an app of your choice.

Add your own app shortcut

Save a link to any HTTPS app or website in Z's hub. These are launch shortcuts, not installed code or permissioned data connections.

Create your Z account

Sign up or log in using your email. Guest mode still works without an account. Account activation needs the Z owner's secure authentication setup; existing chats and notes stay on your device.

Connect your Google account

Connect Google Calendar using a real Google permission window to view upcoming events in Z. Requires a one-time Google OAuth Web Client ID setup. This connection is read-only, and Docs and Classroom are not connected yet.

Privacy & permissions

Plugin preferences, personal tasks and notes currently stay in your browser on this device. Z does not silently read your messages, documents, calendar or school account. Real integrations need permission scopes, encrypted server-side tokens, and a way to disconnect them.
