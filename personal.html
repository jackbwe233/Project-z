    Bring your calendar & schoolwork into Z

    These imports work right now on your iPhone without giving Z access to your Google account. Choose an exported file; Z reads it locally and adds matching tasks.

    To use Google Calendar, export a .ics file. For school assignments, save a spreadsheet with columns Title and Due Date as CSV. Google Classroom does not directly connect yet.

    Import is one-time, not automatic syncing. Reimporting ignores matching tasks. Keep a backup of important data.
