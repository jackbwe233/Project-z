Privacy in Project Z

Local data

Z currently saves chats, notes, tasks, study tools and simulated paper-trading activity in your browser's local storage or browser database. This data does not automatically travel with an account.

Clearing browser data may erase it. Use the app's export functions to keep backups.

Adaptive personalization

Z's communication style learning is off until you turn it on. When enabled, it stores small counts of writing patterns rather than the text of your messages. Memory suggestions about interests and goals require approval unless you separately opt into automatic saving of non-sensitive preferences.

Saved memories can be reviewed, edited, exported, and deleted. When a signed-in account is verified, Z uses a separate local personalization vault for that account. This is not cloud synchronization, and existing chats or other tools may remain accessible to others using the same browser.

Account email

When the owner activates optional sign-up, the email address you provide goes to Supabase's authentication service for account verification and login. Authentication may use cookies or stored session tokens. Do not share magic links.

Without the authentication configuration, the account form is disabled and Z doesn't collect registration emails.

Connected tools

Google Calendar access is separately approved by Google and is currently read-only, unless you choose to copy events into your local Z planner. The stock research tool sends its search terms to an external search provider when you run a search.

Important limitations

There is currently no verified cloud sync, backend account deletion self-service, or unified privacy controls. Before Z is opened for public account registration, the owner should provide a contact for privacy and deletion requests and verify legal obligations.
